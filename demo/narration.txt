Freelancers wait weeks to get paid. Clients pay up front and hope the work shows up. Freelance Escrow fixes both sides with one contract on QDay Aevum. The client locks payment in tUSD8 first, so the freelancer knows the money is real. The freelancer delivers and marks the invoice done. The client approves, and the contract pays out at once. If the work never arrives, the deadline refunds the client. Disputes get three days, and the funds stay locked until they resolve. No middleman, no waiting, no trust needed. The rules are in the contract. Try it on the Aevum testnet; the code is open source, link below.
